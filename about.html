Our Heritage & Craft

The Art of Indian Hospitality

For more than fifteen years, Libin Catering Service & Event Management has transformed celebrations into grand culinary milestones with authentic recipes and silver service.

Generations of Flavor

Where Passion Meets Authentic Tradition

Founded on the principle of serving pure, memorable food, Libin Catering Service & Event Management has grown into one of the most respected catering institutions in South India. Our culinary master chefs preserve traditional hand-ground spice mixes, slow wood-fired dum methods, and age-old culinary secrets passed down through generations.

We believe an extraordinary event begins long before the guests arrive—it starts with selecting fresh country chicken, tender young meats, pure ghee, and crisp farm-grown vegetables. From small family gatherings to mega wedding receptions catering to thousands, every dish is executed with supreme precision.
